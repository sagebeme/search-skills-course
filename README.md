# Find It Free: A Beginner's Course in Searching Well

Big companies make money when you believe the only way to learn is to pay them.
Much of the time that isn't true. Universities, governments, libraries,
researchers and ordinary experts put a huge amount of knowledge online for free,
legally. The hard part is **finding** it and **knowing whether to trust it**.

This course teaches exactly that, in plain words, one small step at a time.

> **Private course.** This folder lives on your own computer only.
> It is not on GitHub and should not be uploaded there.

---

## Who this is for

- Complete beginners. You don't need any tech background.
- Anyone tired of paywalls, "premium" courses and ads pretending to be answers.
- People who want to teach others how to find information on their own.

## What you need

- A phone or computer with a web browser.
- Optional: the Telegram app and a Reddit account (you can read both without one,
  as you'll see).
- A notebook or a text file to keep your exercise answers in.

## Open the course site

There's a fun, clickable version of the course in the `site` folder.

- **Easiest:** double-click `site/index.html`. It opens in your browser, no internet or
  server needed (the fonts need internet; without it the site still works with plainer fonts).
- Write your name on the library card, then press **Stamp my card** at the end of each
  lesson. Your stamps, ticks and notes are saved in that browser only.
- **Changed a lesson?** Run `node site/build.mjs` from this folder so the site picks it up.

## What this course will NOT teach (and why)

Some online communities use clever search tricks for things that hurt people.
This course keeps the useful skills and leaves the harmful uses out:

- **No finding pirated content, cracked software or leaked paid courses.**
  Besides the legal risk, these files are one of the most common ways people
  get infected with malware and lose their accounts or money (see Lesson 06).
  There's almost always a free *legal* version of what you want; Lesson 07
  shows you where.
- **No tracking down or profiling private people ("doxxing").** Searching for a
  real person's home, family or private details can get them hurt. The skills
  here are for finding *knowledge*, not people.
- **No hunting for leaked databases, passwords or login details.** That's
  using stolen information, and it harms the people it was stolen from.

If you hit one of these lines while learning, ask yourself: *"What am I actually
trying to learn?"* Then go find the free, legal source for that. That's what
this whole course is about.

## How to use this course with a group

- Do one lesson per session. Let everyone try the exercise on their own phone
  first, then compare what each person found and how.
- Comparing searches is the fastest way to learn. Two people looking for the
  same thing almost always use different words and get different results.
